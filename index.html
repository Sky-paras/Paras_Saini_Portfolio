// About

The short version of a fairly long resume

I got into analytics through finance, not code. An MBA in Banking & Finance and an early stint as a dealer at SMC Global Securities taught me how numbers move markets and decisions — and how badly most teams need someone to translate raw data into something a stakeholder can actually use. That's what pulled me toward reporting, dashboards, and eventually full BI ownership.

Since then I've built automated MIS dashboards and reporting pipelines across three very different worlds: healthcare diagnostics at Diagnostic Biosystems India, global media analytics at Wipro and Prime Research, and capital markets at SMC. Across all of them the job looks the same underneath — clean the data, model the KPI, automate the pipeline, and tell the story clearly enough that leadership can act on it without a follow-up meeting.

Right now, at Diagnostic Biosystems, that means Power BI dashboards for sales and inventory, Python and Google Apps Script automations that cut reporting turnaround by 30–40%, and CRM/IVR tracking systems built to keep operations honest. Outside work, I keep sharpening the toolkit on public datasets — COVID-19 trends, PhonePe transaction data, EV sales, even Pro Kabaddi stats — because the best way to learn a new technique is to point it at something you're curious about.
